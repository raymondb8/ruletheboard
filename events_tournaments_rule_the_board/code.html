First Tournament?

Everything parents and kids need to know before the clock starts ticking.

What should my child bring?

expand_more
Students should bring a tournament-regulation chess set and clock if they own one, though many events provide them. A healthy snack, water bottle, and a pencil for notation (if required for their section) are also highly recommended.

How long do tournaments last?

expand_more
Scholastic tournaments typically last 4-6 hours depending on the number of rounds and the time control. Most events start at 9:00 AM and conclude by 3:00 PM. We provide a detailed schedule for every specific event on the registration page.

Do I need a US Chess Membership?

expand_more
For "Rated" sections, a USCF membership is mandatory. For "Beginner" or "Unrated" sections, no membership is required. We clearly mark which sections require ratings on the event details page.

Can parents stay in the playing room?

expand_more
To maintain focus and integrity, parents and coaches are usually asked to wait in a designated waiting area once the rounds begin. However, you are always welcome to help your child set up their board before the start of the round.
